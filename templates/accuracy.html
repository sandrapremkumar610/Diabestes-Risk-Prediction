Understanding the Metrics

Key measurements used to evaluate model performance.

Accuracy

Measures the overall percentage of correct predictions.

Precision

Measures how many predicted positive cases were actually positive.

Recall

Measures how many actual positive cases were correctly detected.

F1 Score

Represents the balance between precision and recall.

ROC-AUC

Measures how effectively a model distinguishes between two classes.
